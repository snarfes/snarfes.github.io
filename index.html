About Us

Formosa Running Club is a welcoming group of runners based in Taiwan. Whether you're a beginner or a seasoned athlete, we host weekly sessions including intervals, tempo runs, long runs, and trail adventures across Taipei and beyond. Our mission is to build fitness, friendship, and fun—together.

Training Schedule

Tuesday – Interval Training

Structured speed workouts suitable for all paces.

Thursday – Tempo Run

A steady-paced riverside run designed to build endurance.

Weekend – Long Run / Hike

Explore Taiwan's mountains, trails, and scenic routes.
